Compound words in Swedish

Short answer

Swedish builds new words by joining words together – and writes them as one word: sjuksköterska (nurse), datorskärm (computer screen). Writing them apart is called särskrivning, and it can change the meaning: sjuk sköterska means a sick nurse.

One concept = one word

English often writes compounds as separate words (computer screen, night shift). Swedish doesn't. If two words together describe one thing, write them as one word.

en datorskärm (a computer screen)

en dator skärm

Hon jobbar nattskift. (She works night shifts.)

Hon jobbar natt skift.

Writing apart can change the meaning

  • sjuksköterska (a nurse) – sjuk sköterska (a nurse who is ill)
  • rökfritt (no smoking) – rök fritt (smoke freely)
  • extrapris (special offer) – extra pris (an extra prize)

English loanwords are joined too

en actionfilm, en onlinekurs, en webbsida

en action film, en online kurs

Trick: listen to the stress

Say the word out loud. A Swedish compound has one main stress, usually on the first part: NATTskift, DAtorskärm. If you hear two equally stressed words, they're probably two words.

When to use a hyphen

  • With abbreviations and numbers: tv-program, e-post, 4-åring.
  • When a shared part is left out: barn- och ungdomsverksamhet.

Practice

1. Han jobbar som buss chaufför.

Han jobbar som busschaufför.

2. Vi ska åka på sommar lov.

Vi ska åka på sommarlov.
