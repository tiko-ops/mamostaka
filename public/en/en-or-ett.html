En or ett in Swedish?

Short answer

Most Swedish nouns are en-words – roughly three out of four – so en is the best guess. Nouns ending in -ning, -het, -tion and -are are almost always en-words; nouns ending in -ande, -ende and -um are usually ett-words. After ett, the adjective usually gets a -t: ett stort hus.

Swedish has two genders for nouns, and you can't always tell which one a word has. That's why en or ett is one of the most common mistakes for people learning Swedish. These tips get you most of the way.

When in doubt, guess en

Roughly three out of four Swedish nouns are en-words. If you don't know, en is the safest guess.

Endings that help

  • -ning → en: en tidning (a newspaper), en övning (an exercise)
  • -het → en: en möjlighet (a possibility)
  • -tion, -sion → en: en station, en version
  • -are → en: en lärare (a teacher), en dammsugare (a vacuum cleaner)
  • -ande, -ende → ett: ett meddelande (a message), ett leende (a smile)
  • -um → ett: ett museum, ett datum (a date)

People and animals

Words for people and most animals are en-words: en kvinna, en läkare, en hund, en katt. Important exceptions: ett barn (a child), ett djur (an animal) and ett lejon (a lion).

The adjective changes too

After ett, adjectives usually get a -t at the end. This is a very common mistake, even for advanced learners.

en stor bil – ett stort hus (a big car – a big house)

Bilen är ny. – Huset är nytt. (The car is new. – The house is new.)

Vi har köpt ett stor hus.

Learn the word with its article

Always learn new words as ett bord and en stol, not just bord and stol. When unsure, look the word up in Svenska Akademiens ordlista (SAOL) or Svensk ordbok – they show whether it's en or ett.

Practice

1. ___ tidning

en – words ending in -ning are en-words.

2. ___ meddelande

ett – words ending in -ande are ett-words.

3. Correct it: Det är ett fin dag.

Det är en fin dag. – dag is an en-word.
