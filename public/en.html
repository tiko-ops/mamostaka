    What Mamosta fixes in your Swedish

    • Spelling mistakes – typos and misspelled words.
    • Compound words (särskrivning) – Swedish writes them as one word: sjuksköterska, not sjuk sköterska.
    • Grammar – for example de or dem, en or ett, sin or hans.
    • Commas and capital letters – in Swedish, weekdays, months and languages are written in lowercase.

    Made for people learning Swedish

    Most grammar checkers only fix your text. Mamosta also tells you why, in English, so you make fewer mistakes next time. It's a quick way to proofread a Swedish text before you send it – an email to a colleague, a cover letter, homework for SFI or a message to your landlord.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Mamosta free?

    Yes. Mamosta is completely free and you don't need an account.

    Can it correct my Swedish email, essay or job application?

    Yes. Paste up to 5,000 characters at a time – about one or two pages – and Mamosta corrects spelling, grammar, compound words and punctuation while keeping your style.

    Does it explain my mistakes?

    Yes. Every change gets a short explanation in English, so you learn the rule – for example why it should be de and not dem. You can also choose Arabic, Kurdish or simple Swedish.

    Is my text stored?

    No. Your text is sent encrypted to be corrected and is not stored or logged by Mamosta. There are no cookies.

    How accurate is it?

    Mamosta uses AI and catches most mistakes, but no checker is perfect. Read the result, and undo any change you don't agree with.
